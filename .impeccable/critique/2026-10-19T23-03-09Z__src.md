## Design Specificity Verdict

- **LLM Assessment**: The application establishes a coherent, utilitarian dark-mode terminal environment modeled after professional terminal multiplexers. The strongest visual patterns exist in the split-screen pane rendering and the tab bar. However, secondary panels (`TelemetryManager`, `ConversationHistoryModal`) exhibit generic "AI neon" styling tropes (purple/cyan gradients, glowing borders) that clash with the grounded Termius dark aesthetic.
- **Deterministic Scan**:
  - `src/components/TelemetryManager.jsx:248`: Rule `ai-color-palette` (neon purple/cyan gradient accents on dark backgrounds, a known "AI slop" tell).
- **Verdict**: Distinctive primary workflow, but compromised by generic neon flourishes in extension modals.

## What's Working Well

1. **Ergonomic Split-Screen Multiplexing**: Seamless vertical and horizontal splitting with hotkeys (`Ctrl+Alt+V`, `Ctrl+Alt+H`) and persistent PTY sessions.
2. **1-Click Agent Continuity**: Seamless cataloging of Antigravity sessions with direct resumption into the terminal via `agy --conversation <id>`.
3. **Tab Auto-Naming & Inline Editing**: Shell and process names update tabs dynamically, while double-clicking allows instant custom naming with reset affordance.

## Priority Issues & Recommendations

### [P1] AI Color Palette Tell & Neon Inconsistency
- **Finding**: `TelemetryManager.jsx` and AI history use competing neon purple (`purple-500/600`) and cyan (`cyan-400`) accents on headers and cards, triggering the `ai-color-palette` antipattern.
- **Impact**: Undermines the mature, professional UNIX/Termius aesthetic, making the tooling look like an unpolished AI prototype.
- **Fix**: Unify around Termius brand sapphire blue (`#58a6ff` / `#388bfd`) for general operations and a single restrained amethyst ink (`#bc8cff` / `#8957e5`) reserved strictly for autonomous Antigravity status badges.
- **Suggested Impeccable command**: `impeccable color-contrast` / `impeccable polish`

### [P1] Split-Pane Active Perimeter Focus State
- **Finding**: In vertical and horizontal split modes, active tab focus is signaled primarily by a small "Foco" pill badge and slight text color shift.
- **Impact**: In a multi-pane layout, developers lose rapid spatial orientation of where typing will land.
- **Fix**: Introduce a high-contrast 1px active pane border accent (`border-blue-500/50` with a subtle inner ring glow) to immediately clarify keyboard capture.
- **Suggested Impeccable command**: `impeccable audit`

### [P2] Tab Bar Density & Horizontal Overflow Mask
- **Finding**: The top tab bar horizontally scrolls without a fade mask or overflow dropdown when opening 6+ terminal tabs.
- **Impact**: Tabs truncate abruptly at the right edge without indicating hidden content.
- **Fix**: Apply a CSS linear-gradient mask to the tab container edges and add an overflow chevron menu for quick tab jumping when count > 5.

### [P2] Actionable Empty States for Hosts & Snippets
- **Finding**: When no SSH hosts or snippets exist, the interface displays empty gray text.
- **Impact**: Missed opportunity to onboard users with quick templates (e.g., "Add Localhost", "Add Docker Container", "Add Common Shell Snippet").
- **Fix**: Replace plain empty states with interactive starter cards containing 1-click presets.
